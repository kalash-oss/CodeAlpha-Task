## Tasks

### 1. CGPA Calculator

A C++ program that calculates a student's CGPA based on the grade points and credit hours of their courses.

#### Features

- Takes the number of courses as input
- Accepts grade points and credit hours for each course
- Calculates weighted grade points
- Calculates total credit hours
- Calculates the final CGPA
- Displays course-wise results

#### Formula

```text
CGPA = Total Weighted Grade Points / Total Credit Hours
